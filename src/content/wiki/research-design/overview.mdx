Before any coding or statistics, a study needs a spine: a clear question, a design that can
answer it, and measures you can defend. This module covers that scaffolding — the part every
other module quietly assumes.

## What this module will cover

- Turning a fuzzy interest into an answerable research question.
- Choosing a design (observational, experimental, design-based) that fits the question.
- Sampling: who is in the study, and what you can claim beyond them.
- Validity and reliability — is your measure capturing the right thing, consistently?
