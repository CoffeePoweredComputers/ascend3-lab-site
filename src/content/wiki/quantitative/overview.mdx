Quantitative analysis is about answering questions with numbers — measuring effects,
estimating uncertainty, and reporting results others can reproduce.

## A typical workflow

- Define variables and how each is measured.
- Explore with descriptive statistics and plots.
- Choose a test or model that matches your design and data.
- Report effect sizes and uncertainty, not just p-values.

<Callout type="warning" title="Plan analysis before collecting data">
Deciding your analysis after seeing the data invites p-hacking. Pre-register or at
least write your analysis plan first.
</Callout>

p-value.
