Design-based research builds a tool, activity, or intervention *and* studies it at the same
time — iterating in real classrooms rather than a lab, so what you learn and what you build
improve together. Much of the lab's tool-building work lives here.

## What this module will cover

- What separates design-based research from "just building a tool."
- Design cycles: conjecture → build → deploy → analyze → revise.
- Getting evidence out of iterations without pretending they were a controlled trial.
- Reporting design work so the contribution is legible to reviewers.
