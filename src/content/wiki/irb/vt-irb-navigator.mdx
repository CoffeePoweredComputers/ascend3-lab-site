The [previous lesson](/wiki/irb/do-i-need-irb) helps you reason about *whether* you
need review. This one is the practical follow-up: answer a few questions about
**your** study and it hands you the specific Virginia Tech forms, templates, and
links to act on — geared to social, behavioral, and computing research.

<IRBNavigator />

<Callout type="warning" title="Guidance, not a determination">
This walkthrough is a starting point, not an official ruling. Only the
[HRPP](https://www.research.vt.edu/sirc/hrpp/index.html) can classify your study —
and even "exempt" research needs a determination *from* them. When in doubt, email
**irb@vt.edu** or use their office hours before you submit.
</Callout>

Once you know your path, the [Writing a Protocol](/wiki/irb/writing-a-protocol)
lesson walks through filling out the template you just downloaded.
