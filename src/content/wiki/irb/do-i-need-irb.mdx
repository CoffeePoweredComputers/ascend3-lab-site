Most projects fall into one of three buckets: **not human-subjects research**,
**exempt**, or **requiring review**. Work through the tree below for a first read —
it is guidance, not an official determination.

<DecisionTree
  start="q1"

        { label: 'Yes', to: 'q3' },

<Callout type="warning" title="You don't self-exempt">
Even "exempt" research requires a determination *from* the IRB. Researchers don't
get to declare their own studies exempt.
</Callout>
