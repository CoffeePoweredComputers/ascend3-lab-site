payoff, side by side.

## Same data, two valid readings

The two paths reach a **matched theme for each phenomenon** — built from the *same
interviews* — but frame it differently. Read each row across: descriptive and reliably
codable on the left, interpretive and researcher-constructed on the right.

<Callout type="tip" title="The lesson of the whole module">
Method is a choice with consequences. The codebook path buys you transferability and a
reliability claim; the reflexive path buys you interpretive depth. Choose the one your
question and epistemology call for — and report it honestly <Cite id="braun-clarke-2021-onesize" />
