Mixed-methods research pairs numbers with narrative — using qualitative and quantitative
strands together so each covers the other's blind spots. It assumes the
[Qualitative](/wiki/qualitative/overview) and [Quantitative](/wiki/quantitative/overview)
basics and focuses on how to combine them.

## What this module will cover

- When mixing actually helps — and when it is just more work.
- Common designs: convergent, explanatory-sequential, exploratory-sequential.
- Integrating findings with joint displays, not two disconnected results sections.
