Last week was the queue. This week we keep the same chain and allow changes anywhere in it. Step 1. In Week 8, a linked queue kept two references. dequeue removed the node at the front through firstNode, and enqueue added a node at the back through lastNode. Nothing in the middle of the chain was ever touched. Step 2. A list keeps the same kind of chain, but every entry has a position. The 9.1 videos count positions from 0, the way an array does, so A is at position 0. Step 3. A list can add or remove at any position. To put X at position 1, the node at position 0 has to change its next. That is today’s idea: on a singly linked chain, you work through the node before the position. Step 4. These are the list’s operations, from the ListInterface in the module. Four of them take a position. add with one argument adds at the end. contains, getLength, isEmpty, clear and toArray work on the whole list. A queue adds only at the end and removes only at position 0, so it is a list that only uses two of the positions. Part 1 starts with add at position 0.
