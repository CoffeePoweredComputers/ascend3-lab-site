The costs in one table

Two compact tables arriving in turn, then two footnotes. Left, Stack: push, pop and peek are each O of 1 for the array and for the chain. Right, Bag: add and remove with no argument are O of 1; contains, getFrequencyOf, remove of an entry and toArray are O of n; the same for the array and for the chain. Each cost is a coloured badge, blue for O of 1 and maroon for O of n. The footnotes: an array that can grow makes add O of n on the call that grows it; a top at index 0 makes pop and push O of n.

The reference card; skip the walk-through if the clock is short. Step 1: the stack. Both of the module’s designs give push, pop and peek in constant time, because both keep the top where nothing has to move. Step 2: the bag from Weeks 3 and 4. add and the no-argument remove are one move, because a bag has no order and the end is always free; anything that has to find an entry, or visit every entry, is linear, on the array and on the chain alike. Step 3: the two exceptions today found. An array that grows makes add O(n) on the call that grows it; a top at index 0 makes the stack shift. Next: the homework.
