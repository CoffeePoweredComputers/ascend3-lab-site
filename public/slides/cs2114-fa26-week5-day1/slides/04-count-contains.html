This is the contains the room chose in Week 3, option A, and the only new thing is the number on the right. Say what we are counting: a move is one thing the computer does that we care about — here, one call to equals. Step 1: found false, i zero. Steps 2 to 5: press once per entry — ant, bee, cat, dog — and watch the counter; gnu is not in the bag, so no compare ever succeeds and the loop only stops when i reaches the count. Ask before step 5: what stops it? Step 6: return false; four entries, four compares. Ask: and if gnu were at index 0? One compare — the best case. We count the worst case, because that is the one that grows. Step 7: the same call on eight entries costs eight. The moves grow with n, and the word for that is linear.
