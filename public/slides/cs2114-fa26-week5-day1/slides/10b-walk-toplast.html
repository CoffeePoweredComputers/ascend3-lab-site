A walkthrough of pop for an array stack with the top at the last occupied index, under a program-counter bar: check for an empty stack, read stack at topIndex, clear that slot, decrement topIndex, return. Read the top: stack[topIndex] is E.

Answer: A — and there is no loop to walk. Step 1: the empty check, one compare. Step 2: read the top, E. Step 3: clear the slot. Step 4: topIndex comes down — watch the marker move, not the entries. D is the top now and it never moved. Step 5: four moves, whatever the count. O(1). Say the mirror: push writes at topIndex plus one and counts up — O(1) too.
