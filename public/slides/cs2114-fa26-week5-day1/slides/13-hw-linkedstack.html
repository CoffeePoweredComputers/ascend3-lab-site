The homework is three methods on the class we just popped from. The chain on the left is the test case to keep in your head: A pushed first, C on top. Step 1: toString — the entries top-down with no separators, so CBA; an empty stack gives the empty string. Step 2: toArray — a new array with the top at index 0, so C, B, A. Step 3: getBaseOfStack — the entry at the bottom, A, or null when the stack is empty. All three are one walk: start at topNode, follow getNext until null — Week 4’s move.
