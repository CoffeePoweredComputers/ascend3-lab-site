A, C and E must be leaves

The answer to Vote 2, built in ten presses. Left, the inorder listing A, B, C, D, E as five cells; right, a tree built from it with level labels 1 to 4. B is the root. In an inorder listing, everything before the root is in its left subtree and everything after it is in its right subtree.

Only A comes before B, so A on its own is B’s left subtree, on level 2.

The height is exactly 3, and the left side reaches only level 2. So C, D and E fill levels 2 and 3 on the right, with one node on level 2 and two under it on level 3.

If C were B’s right child, nothing could go to C’s left, because no letter lies between B and C in the listing. D and E would both go right, and E would land on level 4.

If E were B’s right child, C and D would both go to E’s left, and C would land on level 4.

With D as B’s right child, C is D’s left child and E is D’s right child. The listing still reads A B C D E, and the height is 3.

Option C is correct: A, C and E must be leaves.

Options A and D include B, but B is the root and has two children, so it is not a leaf.

Options B and E include D, which has two children, C and E, so it is not a leaf either.

  5. D

Step 1: B is the root, as the question says. In an inorder listing every node of the left subtree is printed before the root and every node of the right subtree after it, so A is on the left and C, D and E are on the right. Step 2: one node on the left: A is B’s left child. Step 3: the tree has height 3, and A is on level 2, so the three nodes on the right have to reach level 3 and may not go below it. One of them is B’s right child on level 2, and the other two sit on level 3 under it. Step 4: try C as that right child. In the listing nothing comes between B and C, so C’s left subtree is empty, D and E both go to C’s right, and one of them ends up on level 4. Too tall. Step 5: E on top is the mirror image: C and D both go left, and C ends up on level 4. Step 6: D on top puts C on its left and E on its right, both on level 3. Read it back in order: A, B, C, D, E. It is the only shape that works. Step 7: the leaves are A, C and E, option C. Step 8: options A and D include B, which has two children. Step 9: options B and E include D, which also has two children. Step 10: C.
