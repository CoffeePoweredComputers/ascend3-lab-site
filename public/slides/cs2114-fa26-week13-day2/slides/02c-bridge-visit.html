Day 1 was about the shape of a binary tree. Step 1: this is the tree from the module video. On Day 1, getNumberOfNodes made one call per node, and each call added up what its children reported; the height method did the same with a maximum. Step 2: today the calls are the same, but each one also visits its node, which here means printing its letter. Where the visit sits among the two recursive calls decides the order in which the letters come out. Step 3: ask the room for a guess, with no discussion yet: if every call prints its own letter before it calls on its children, which letter comes out last? Part 1 answers it on the first walk.
