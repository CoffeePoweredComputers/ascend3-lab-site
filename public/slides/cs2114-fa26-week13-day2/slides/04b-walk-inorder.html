Inorder visits each node between its two subtrees

A walkthrough of inorder on the same tree, under a program-counter bar. The editor holds inorder of node: if node is not null, the visit line sits between the call on the left child and the call on the right child. On the right, the tree from the previous slide with a blue ring that moves to each node as it is printed, and an output plate underneath. inorder: visit between the two calls

  1. void inorder(BinaryNode<T> node) {
  2. if (node != null) {
  3. inorder(node.getLeftChild());
  4. visit(node.getData());
  5. inorder(node.getRightChild());
  6. }
  7. }

A’s call goes left to B, and B’s call goes left to D. D has no left child, so D is printed first.

D has no right child, so D’s call returns. B’s left call is finished, so B is printed.

B’s call goes right to E, and E’s call goes left to F. F has no left child, so F is printed.

F’s call returns, and E is printed between its two calls.

E’s call goes right to G. G has no children, so G is printed.

G, E and B return. A’s left subtree is finished, so A is printed.

A’s call goes right to C. C has no left child, so C is printed.

C’s call goes right to H. H has no children, so H is printed.

The left subtree first, then the root, then the right subtree: D B F E G A C H.

Same tree, same method, with the visit line moved between the two recursive calls. The call stack is drawn as on the previous slide. Step 1: a call goes left before it visits, so the first letter printed is the leftmost node, D, reached by going left from A and then from B. The stack holds three calls, and A and B are both waiting on line 3; neither has printed yet. Step 2: D’s right call gets null and returns, B’s left call is done, and B is printed. Step 3: B goes right to E, E goes left to F, and F is printed. Step 4: E is printed between F and G. Step 5: G. Step 6: G, E and B return, and only now is A printed, after every node of its left subtree. The stack is back down to A’s call alone. Step 7: C has no left child, so C is printed straight away. Step 8: H. Step 9: D B F E G A C H. Each node is printed after everything in its left subtree and before everything in its right subtree. Next, the visit line moves once more, to the bottom.
