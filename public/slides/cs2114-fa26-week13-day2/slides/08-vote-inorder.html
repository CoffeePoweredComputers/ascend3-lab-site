Given a binary tree of height 3 with 5 nodes and the inorder traversal ABCDE, which of the following must be leaves if B is the root?

  1. A

    A, B

  2. B

    A, D, E

  3. C

    A, C, E

  4. D

    D, B

  5. E

    D

Answer: C — A, C, E [derived by the deck; confirm in Runestone’s results view before class.
