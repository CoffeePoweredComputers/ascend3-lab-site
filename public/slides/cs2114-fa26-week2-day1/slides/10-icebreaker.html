Icebreaker — groups of 3 or 4

These are the people you will be voting with all semester.

Four prompts numbered down the left, arriving one at a time: your name, your major, the coding you have done, and what you want from this class. At right, four people around a table with a block of ice in the middle, each holding up a little wooden-handled hammer. As each prompt arrives, one of them brings the hammer down on the ice: a few blue chips fly off and fall, and a crack draws from that side of the ice to its centre, so by the fourth prompt the ice is in four pieces.

  1. your name
  2. your major
  3. the coding you have done
  4. what you want from this class
Three minutes, no more. Walk the room. Step through the four prompts as you read them out — one blow of the hammer each — then leave the slide up. Go round the group on each prompt rather than letting one person answer all four. The fourth prompt is the useful one — it gives them something to say to each other that is not small talk, and it gives you the temperature of the room. These groups are the ones they will vote with for the rest of the semester, so it is worth the three minutes.
