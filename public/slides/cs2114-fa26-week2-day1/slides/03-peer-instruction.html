PEER INSTRUCTION

What were your best and worst learning experiences?

Two rows of words gathered from students. First the worst-class words, in muted stone: boring, no interaction, no help, no feedback, too hard, uncaring. Then the best-class words, in orange: active, immediate feedback, deliberate practice, social, fun, caring.

Worst

  • boring
  • no interaction
  • no help
  • no feedback
  • too hard
  • uncaring

Best

  • active
  • immediate feedback
  • deliberate practice
  • social, fun, caring
Ask the room before revealing either row — take three or four answers for “worst”, then three or four for “best”. The point of the pair: nobody ever names “a very clear lecture” as their best learning experience. Peer instruction is the cheapest way to buy the whole second row at once.
