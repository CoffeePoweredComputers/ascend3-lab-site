Same transcript, our turn — say that the answer is the course’s design, not a chatbot’s. The projects are scoped so that a model cannot simply produce them: they are design problems, and what it hands you is a component, not the design. That is on purpose. It means anyone who does use one here is using it on something genuinely complex, which is how industry uses it — deciding what to build, checking what comes back, fitting the pieces together. Point three is the fun one: with a model handling the pieces, you get to build things bigger and cooler than a second-semester student normally could, and see them work. That joy is the point of the course. Tie it back to the previous slide: the risks were real, and every one of them is a risk of using it on work that is too easy.
