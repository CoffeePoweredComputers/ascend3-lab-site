Same clock as the start of the hour — say that out loud. Three clocks: Friday for everyone; Sunday for everyone; lab day for each section, so the date is a different day for each of them — and from here on, every week, which is a rule rather than a date. Say the lab-day ones last so they are the thing they walk out with. Homework Week 2 is in PrairieLearn after the modules; Modules 3.x are due Sunday. Challenge Exercise 01 is due before your lab section, and the UTAs are in the room to help you while you are there. Project 1 is released this week and its scoping document is due before your lab section next week — the same window, one week on; read the spec before lab, because the scoping doc is where the design questions start.
