Week 2, on three different clocks

A two-week axis, this week on the left and next week on the right. First a maroon-orange Homework bar runs from Monday and stops at Friday, with a pole rising at the Friday tick. Then the Challenge Exercise 01 bar runs solid through Monday and fades across Monday to Friday, with a range line under the axis: it is due before your lab section, and lab sections run all week. Then a lab room is drawn above the axis — four students seated and one UTA standing among them — and attendance becomes required from this week on. Last, an orange bar runs from this Monday all the way to next Friday, with a pole at that tick: Project 1 is released this week, and its first deliverable, the scoping document, is due next Friday.

Logistics first, then the reason we run class the way we do. Step 1 — Homework Week 2, in PrairieLearn after the modules, due Friday: one date, the same for everyone. Step 2 — Challenge Exercise 01, submitted to Web-CAT from Eclipse, due before your lab section: say “your lab day” out loud and point at the faded stretch, because the date is a different day for each of them. Step 3 — attendance was optional in Week 1 and is required from here on; the UTAs are in the room to help you while you are in it. Step 4 — Project 1 is released this week and its first deliverable, the scoping document, is due next Friday: it is the long bar because it starts now, not next week, and it is orange because that is Project 1’s colour for the semester. Read the spec before lab. Announcements land on Piazza first: turn instructor-post notifications on before you leave.
