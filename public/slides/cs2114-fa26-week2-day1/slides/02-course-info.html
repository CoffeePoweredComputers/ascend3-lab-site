Week 2 and 3 Overview

A two-week axis, this week on the left and next week on the right. First a maroon-orange Homework bar runs from Monday and stops at Friday, with a pole rising at the Friday tick. Then a blue Modules 3.x bar runs on to Sunday, with its own pole. Then the Challenge Exercise 01 bar runs solid through Monday and fades across Monday to Friday, with a range line under the axis: it is due before your lab section, and lab sections run all week. Then a lab room is drawn above the axis — four students seated and one UTA standing among them — and attendance becomes required from this week on. Last, an orange Project 1 bar runs from this Monday, solid through next Monday and fading across next week, with a range line under next week: Project 1 is released this week, and its first deliverable, the scoping document, is due before your lab section next week.

Logistics first, then the reason we run class the way we do. Three clocks: Friday, Sunday, and your lab day. Step 1 — Homework Week 2, in PrairieLearn after the modules, due Friday: one date, the same for everyone. Step 2 — Modules 3.x, due Sunday, the same clock as last week’s 1.x and 2.x. Step 3 — Challenge Exercise 01, due before your lab section: say “your lab day” out loud and point at the faded stretch, because the date is a different day for each of them. Step 4 — attendance was optional in Week 1 and is required from here on; the UTAs are in the room to help you while you are in it. Step 5 — Project 1 is released this week and its first deliverable, the scoping document, is due before your lab section next week — the same window as the challenge exercise, one week later, and orange because that is Project 1’s colour for the semester. It is called Learning to Scope for a reason: read the spec before lab and start the day it is released. Announcements land on Piazza first: turn instructor-post notifications on before you leave.
