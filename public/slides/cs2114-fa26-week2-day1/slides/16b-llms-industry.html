Where these tools sit in the work

Three lines building to a claim: using LLMs to design, write and test software is now ordinary in industry; used judiciously they are valuable tools; and being effective with one requires understanding the language and the concepts yourself. Then, large, what this course is actually for.

  • Using LLMs to design, write and test software is ordinary in industry now.

So this course teaches design, development, and above all the critical thinking you will need — whatever the language, whatever the tool.

Do not open this as a warning. The honest framing is that the tools are here, they are useful, and the thing that makes them useful is the thing this course builds. The third bullet is the hinge — the rest of the arc is evidence for it.
