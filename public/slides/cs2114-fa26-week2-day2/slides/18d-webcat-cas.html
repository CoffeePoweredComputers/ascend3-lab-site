Step 3 of the Web-CAT guide, and the step where submissions get lost: the sign-in cannot finish inside Eclipse's embedded window, so a browser opens and asks to be reopened outside it. Two failure modes to name out loud: cookies blocked in the browser makes the CAS login loop without ever succeeding, and closing the browser at the “received and processing” message is fine — but closing it before the confirmation means nothing was submitted. The confirmation message on card C is the receipt; until it appears, the submission has not happened.
