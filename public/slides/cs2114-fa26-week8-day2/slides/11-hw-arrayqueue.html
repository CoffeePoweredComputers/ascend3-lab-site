PrairieLearn › Week 8: Queues — Coding Practice · best 4 of 8 count · due Friday

This week’s homework has eight problems at 10 points each. The best four count, and they are due Friday October 16 at 11:59 PM on PrairieLearn. Last class showed the four LinkedQueue problems; these are the four on an array. All four give students an ArrayQueue that works like the one on today’s slides, with contents, frontIndex, backIndex and one unused slot. It is fixed-size, so when the queue is full, checkCapacity throws an exception instead of growing the array. The drawing shows a full queue: frontIndex 4, backIndex 2, slot 3 unused. Step 1, Bad Enqueue 1: an enqueue with one mistake, and the task is to find it. Ask where backIndex is when the entry is written. Step 2, Bad Enqueue 2: a second enqueue with a different mistake. Ask which of the two indices an enqueue should change. Step 3, Manipulate 1: the queue holds red, red, red. Using only enqueue and dequeue, it has to end as red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet. Six entries fill a queue of capacity 6, so the order of the calls matters. Step 4, Manipulate 2: the queue holds 13 down to 7, seven entries in a queue of capacity 7, and has to end as 9 down to 3.
