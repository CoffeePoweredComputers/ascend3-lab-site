This week’s homework has eight problems at 10 points each; your best four count, due Friday October 16 at 11:59 PM on PrairieLearn. Last class showed the four LinkedQueue problems; these are the four on an array. All four hand you an ArrayQueue that works the way today’s slides do, with contents, frontIndex, backIndex and one unused slot; it is fixed-size, so a full queue makes checkCapacity throw instead of growing. The drawing is a full one: front on 4, back on 2, slot 3 unused. Step 1 — Bad Enqueue 1: someone else’s enqueue with a mistake; find it. Ask where backIndex points at the moment the entry is written. Step 2 — Bad Enqueue 2: their second try, a different mistake; which of the two indices should an enqueue change? Step 3 — Manipulate 1: the queue holds red, red, red, and you may only call enqueue and dequeue to end with red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet. Six entries in a queue of capacity 6 is full, so plan the order of your calls. Step 4 — Manipulate 2: the queue holds 13 down to 7, seven entries in a queue of capacity 7, and has to end as 9 down to 3.
