Answer: E — five elements in the queue [key from the PrairieLearn twin; confirm in Runestone’s results view]. Step 1: capacity 5 with one unused entry is an array of six slots, 0 to 5. Put the two indices on it: front on 3, back on 1. Back is before front, so the queue wraps. Steps 2 to 6: walk from the front, one slot per press: 3, 4, 5, then (5 + 1) % 6 is 0, then 1, which is the back. Five entries. Slot 2 is the one left over, the unused slot. Step 7: the full test from Part 1 says the same thing: back plus 2 is 3, mod 6 still 3, which is front. Step 8: the wrong answers. Four comes from stopping at slot 4 or from counting 3 to 1 the short way as if there were no slot 5. Six would need every slot, and this design always leaves one empty. Invalid: both indices are legal, and a full queue is a legal state. Empty, one and three do not match the count. Step 9: the verdict. Runestone activity vtcs2_circularArray_full.
