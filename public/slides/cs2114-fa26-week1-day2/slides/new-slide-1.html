Projects 1–3 · teams of 3–4

Even more important for the projects

Same three habits as the last slide; on a project each one counts more. Step 1 — start early: a project is not one deadline but three — Scope, Spec, Final for Project 1 — and each is due before your lab section. Step 2 — each deliverable is built on the last: the scope decides what is in and out, the spec draws it, the final builds it. Project 1 is called "Learning to Scope" for a reason: define before you build. Step 3 — and you do not own the clock: three or four of you stand at "released" together.
