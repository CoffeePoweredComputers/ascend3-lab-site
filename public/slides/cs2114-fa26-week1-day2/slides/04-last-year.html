REFLECTIONS FROM THE 2025 CLASSES

We asked last year’s students what they’d tell you

A project clock: a horizontal axis from released on the left to due on the right. A start marker sits just after released, and an orange bar — the time you have — runs from it all the way to due. Then the marker slides right, to just before due, leaving a dashed ghost where it was, and the orange bar drains from the left down to a short stub. The bare axis between the ghost and the marker is time that passed unused. The most common regret from the 2025 classes, by a wide margin, was starting late.
