Being a good collaborator

  1. 01Start when the team starts
  2. 02Say “I’m stuck” the same day
  3. 03Keep your work visible and stay in communication
  4. 04Own your part(s), know the whole

and be kind, considerate, and helpful!

Projects 1–3 · teams of 3–4. Step 1 — Start when the team starts: your piece is someone else's input; late for you is late for all of you (the ring). Step 2 — Say "I'm stuck" the same day: bad news early is a plan, bad news on the due date is an apology (the downstream arrow turns orange — that person can now plan). Step 3 — Keep your work visible: small, frequent commits to the team repo; nobody can help with code that lives only on your laptop (the pieces leave the seats for the table). Step 4 — Own your part, know the whole: read the whole spec and your teammates' code; a team that can explain every class ships a design, not a pile. This is the short version — you will learn a lot more about this as the semester goes on; teams re-form for every project.
