The rules for next class. Test 2 is in lecture, Wednesday for MW and Thursday for TR, in your own section’s room. It is a Canvas quiz inside LockDown Browser. It is 45 minutes long, you get one attempt, and it has about 19 questions. It is closed book: no notes, no IDE, no web, and no talking. Only your laptop is allowed, not your phone, so do the Canvas 2-factor sign-in before you start and then put the phone away. Pen and paper for scratch work are allowed. Put your student ID on the desk. Step 1, DO: charge your laptop, bring your student ID, have LockDown Browser installed and tested, and run Test #2 Practice once more tonight. Step 2, DON’T: do not open Eclipse or any other program, do not use your phone, do not discuss the test until both sections have taken it, and do not leave before the end of the period. The Test 2 Redo is Wednesday November 4 for MW and Thursday November 5 for TR, in lecture.
