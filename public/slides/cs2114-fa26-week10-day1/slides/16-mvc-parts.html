Before the calendar, a short look at MVC, because Project 3 is Queue + MVC and the scope is due at your lab this week. This is a six-file example: Counter, Observer, ConsoleView, WindowView, CounterController and Runner. Step 1. Counter is the model. It has an int called data, a list of Observers, increment, getData, addObserver and notifyObservers. increment adds one to data and then calls notifyObservers, which calls update on every observer in the list. Counter has no window and no println. Step 2. The two views. ConsoleView prints the number. WindowView extends JFrame and shows the number on a label, with an Increment button under it. Both implement the Observer interface, which has one method, update of int data. The model calls update on an Observer, so it does not know or care whether that observer is a window or a console. Step 3. CounterController holds the model. Its one method, onIncrementClicked, calls model.increment. The button in WindowView calls controller.onIncrementClicked when it is clicked. Step 4. The arrows. A click goes to the controller, the controller calls increment on the model, and the model calls update on every view. In Runner, one Counter, one controller, two WindowViews and one ConsoleView are created, and all three views are added to the same model as observers. Project 3 uses this structure with a queue. Deliverable 1, the scope, is due at your lab this week. Deliverable 2, the spec, is due at your Week 11 lab, and it has to show which classes are the model and which are the views.
