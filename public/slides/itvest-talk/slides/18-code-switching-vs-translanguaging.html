Two theories of multilingual cognition

How do bilinguals draw on their languages? Two competing theories.

theory 01
Code-switching
Alternating between separate language systems — like toggling radio stations between English and Spanish.1

"नमस्ते" ⇄ "Hello"

theory 02
Translanguaging
Drawing from one unified linguistic repertoire — selecting features fluidly without regard for named-language boundaries.2

"नमस्ते" + "Hello" + "কেমন আছেন?" = one repertoire

Translanguaging is the framing I find more powerful — and, honestly, more interesting.
