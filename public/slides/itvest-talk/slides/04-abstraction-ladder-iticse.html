[0:15 → 0:40 · budget 0:25 · 5 clicks] Quick framing. Programming has always climbed an abstraction ladder, one rung at a time: (1) Assembly — register-level, no abstraction over the machine. (2) C and FORTRAN — structured, portable. (3) Java and C++ — objects and types. (4) Python — readable, dominant in CS1 today. Each rung trades constraint for accessibility. (5) With GenAI we've stepped onto the top rung, and it's qualitatively different: natural language. The constraint system is gone; ambiguity is the default. That's the "Prompts First" moment — and the classroom question it leaves is: how do you teach, and grade, programming in natural language? That question is this talk.
