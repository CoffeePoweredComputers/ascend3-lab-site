[0:40 · 3 clicks · opening out before the platform slide] Four problem types isn't a ceiling, it's what we've built. Three things vary independently, and they vary at different points in the SAME pipeline. (1) What we put in front of the student — code, examples, a sketch, a behaviour description, half an implementation. Each one trains a different habit. And how the student answers back — which is most of this talk: natural language in any language, diagrams, structured templates. (2) And at the other end, how we judge it. We use test correctness because it's cheap, but it's the crudest signal on the list. Segmentation, abstraction level, how many times they revised, how far the prompt sits from an expert's — all sitting there unused. The pipeline is the reusable thing. Which is why it needs somewhere to live.
