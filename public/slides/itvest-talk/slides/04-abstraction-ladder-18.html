Framing · 03

The slow climb toward prompting in plain language

We have always pursued better abstractions. Each rung trades formal constraint for accessibility.

Natural language is qualitatively different — the constraint system disappears.

Reeves et al. trace this same arc and argue we've reached a "Prompts First" moment — the way the field once arrived at "Objects First."1

Most of what follows is what I think that looks like in CS1.

Assembly
register-level; no abstraction over the machine.
C · FORTRAN
structured, compiled; portable across machines.
Java · C++
objects and types; managed or manual memory.
Python
readable, batteries included; dominant in CS1 today.
The cluster
partial bridges — accessible, but constraint still binding.
Scratch Spreadsheets SQL DSLs · Inform 7 · Gherkin
the constraint system disappears here
Natural language
unconstrained · ambiguity is the default.
