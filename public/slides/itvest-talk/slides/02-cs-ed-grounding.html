What is Computing Education?

it is
Discipline-based education research
A research field focused on how people learn computing — adjacent to physics education research and math education research. Theory-building and empirical, not curricular.
it is not
EdTech  ·  "programming education"
Not building tools for sale, not the practice of teaching code. Distinct enterprise with its own methods, journals, and concerns.

My focus: at-scale assessment — both formative and summative — in introductory computing.
