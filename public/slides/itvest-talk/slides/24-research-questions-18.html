What’s next

Research questions going forward

RQ1: Barriers
What difficulties do learners have when programming in natural language — across modalities and L1s?
RQ2: Scaffolds
What feedback, hints, and structures help learners overcome those difficulties at scale?
RQ3: Transfer
Do these activities prepare students for authentic interactions with production GenAI coding assistants?
RQ4: Cognition
What mental models — and misconceptions — do students form when "programming" at this level of abstraction?
RQ5: Equity
How do we design natural-language programming activities that leverage — rather than suppress or restrict — learners' full linguistic repertoires?
