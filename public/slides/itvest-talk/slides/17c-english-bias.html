A long-standing bias

Programming has been an English-language activity

The history of programming and computing is, overwhelmingly, the history of writing in English.1

language design
Keywords are English
if, while, return, class — every mainstream language assumes English literacy as a prerequisite.
documentation
Stack Overflow, MDN, official docs
The world's body of programming knowledge is overwhelmingly English-first; translations lag the source, often by years.
pedagogy
Textbooks and tooling
CS1 curricula, error messages, even most variable-naming conventions presume English as the working language.
an easy assumption

It would be easy to assume natural-language programming inherits the same bias — that once GenAI is in the loop, English is just the default.

but it doesn't

GenAI models are trained on text in hundreds of languages. That brings a new pedagogical need into computing education — one we haven't been forced to take seriously before: linguistically aware pedagogy.

  1. 1See Guo, P. J. (2018). Non-Native English Speakers Learning Computer Programming. CHI 2018 — for an empirical look at how much friction English-only computing introduces.
