[1:10 · 2 clicks · closes Branch 2 — the warrant for 11b] Say the number honestly. 81% is FUNCTION-EXTRACTION accuracy: we took ad-hoc decomposition diagrams from 133 CS1 students doing Evil Hangman, fed them straight to GPT-4.1, and checked by hand whether the functions the student drew showed up in the generated code. Four out of five did. That is not "the model grades diagrams correctly 81% of the time" — don't say that. It's the precondition: the model reads a messy student drawing reliably enough to act on it. Grading and feedback are what that makes possible, and that's the work in flight. Formative, no control condition, one problem. Say so before someone asks.
