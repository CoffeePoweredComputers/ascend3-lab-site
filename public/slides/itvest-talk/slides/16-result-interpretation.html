Act III · skill 02

Why result interpretation matters

A student who can write a spec but can't tell whether the answer's any good has outsourced something they should be doing themselves.

recognize
Right output
When does the answer match the spec?
recognize
Wrong output
When does it visibly fail? Edge cases, type errors, off-by-ones.
recognize
Confidently wrong
When does it produce plausible-looking nonsense? The hard case.

Probeable Problems get students inferring behavior from outputs. Refute Problems get them looking for counterexamples. Either way: testing is part of the job, not something we tack on later.
