Act II · activities 03 + 04

Probeable & Refute Problems

Two activities focused on result interpretation — the skill of reading a system's behavior and deciding when to trust it.

probeable
Infer the spec by probing
Students are given a black-box function. They craft inputs, observe outputs, and reconstruct what the function does.

f(3) → 9
f(-2) → 4
f("a") → error

"...squares its numeric argument."

refute
Disprove a claim
Students are given a claim about a piece of code. They construct a counterexample — an input on which the claim fails.

"This function returns the maximum of two numbers."

max(-3, -7) → -7 // refuted

Both are testing knowledge in disguise — and that's the point. Whether a student really benefits from GenAI usually comes down to whether they can tell a good answer from a bad one.
