My current work · India trip

CS instruction in linguistically diverse classrooms — starting with India.

Part of my research goes here: how do you teach programming where the medium of instruction, students' first languages, and the instructor's own languages may not line up?

The objective

Visit a place where instructors must draw on their full linguistic repertoire to meet students from a wide variety of backgrounds and abilities.

What I did

Three weeks across seven institutions. Talks, Purplex demos, conversations with faculty about how they actually teach.
