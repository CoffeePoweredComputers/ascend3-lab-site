Agency

Talk track: responsibility is boxes piled into your wheelbarrow — each one limits how freely you can roam. Everyone in this room has a different load already: a visa, student loan debt, family responsibilities. But everyone here is young, and whatever agency you have, it is likely the most you will ever have in your life. The career decisions you make now are the most impactful you will ever make. Step through boxes 1–5 (the bar drains with each), land the takeaway on step 6, then hit step 7 — the beard goes gray — and let it sit: this is a whole career going by with the barrow still in hand.
