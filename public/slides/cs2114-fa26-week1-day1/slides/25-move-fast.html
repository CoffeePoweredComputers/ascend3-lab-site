Move fast and break things.

— Facebook’s internal motto, 2009–2014

  • Move fast — To where?
  • Break things — what are we breaking? Who owns it? Who is impacted?
Read the motto out loud with the weight on “and” — the speed and the breakage were always sold as a package, not a trade-off anyone agreed to. Then take it apart slowly; leave a beat between each question. The last one is the whole slide: “things” is a euphemism, and it is doing a lot of work. Connect it straight back to von Braun — same dodge, better branding.
