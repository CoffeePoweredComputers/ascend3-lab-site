Grading: Per Deliverable, EMRN Rubric

E · Exceeds

10 pts

Beyond Meets, notably strong.

Delete asks first — and offers undo.

M · Meets

8.5 pts

Does what’s asked, done well. The target.

Tap delete — the task is gone.

R · Revisable

7.5 pt

Close — fix it in the revision window.

Deletes, but the count goes stale.

N · Not Yet

5 pts

Not enough to assess yet.

Tap delete… chirp… chirp…

Note: If you get R and N on a rubric item your team may address it in the next deliverable and get it raised to a "Meets".
