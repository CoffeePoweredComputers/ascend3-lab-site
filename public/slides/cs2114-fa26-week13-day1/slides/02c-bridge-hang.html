Last week we kept a sorted list in an array and in a chain. Step 1. This is a sorted chain of five nodes. Each node has one next reference, so from firstNode there is exactly one way to walk it: 3, 7, 10, 12, 20, then null. Step 2. A tree node has two references, a left and a right. The same five values can hang from 10: 3 on its left and 12 on its right, then 7 on the right of 3 and 20 on the right of 12. From 10 there are now two ways to go. The values happen to be placed so that smaller values sit to the left and larger to the right; that is the binary search tree of Week 14, and today we do not use it. Step 3.
