In the call for i = 2, unsorted = data[2] holds 12, and j is 1.

64 > 12, so 64 is copied into slot 2, and j becomes 0.

25 > 12, so 25 is copied into slot 1, and j becomes −1.

j >= 0 is false, so data[0] = unsorted puts 12 in slot 0. The array reads 12 25 64 22 11.

In the call for i = 3, unsorted holds 22, and j is 2.

64 > 22, so 64 is copied into slot 3, and j becomes 1.

25 > 22, so 25 is copied into slot 2, and j becomes 0.

12 > 22 is false, so the loop ends and data[1] = unsorted puts 22 in slot 1. The array reads 12 22 25 64 11.

For i = 4, unsorted holds 11. 64, 25, 22 and 12 are all larger than 11, so each is copied one slot right, and j ends at −1.

data[0] = unsorted puts 11 in slot 0. The array reads 11 12 22 25 64.

Ten compares and nine shifts in all. Each pass compares the held value with the sorted entries to its left, starting from the right, until it reaches one that is not larger.
