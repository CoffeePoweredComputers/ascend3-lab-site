This week’s homework on PrairieLearn has eight problems at 10 points each, and your best four count. It is due Friday October 16 at 11:59 PM. The first four problems are on a linked queue, which is today’s topic. The other four are on an array queue, which is next class. The queue on the left is the example that the problems use: 1, 2, 3, with firstNode at 1 and lastNode at 3. Step 1. LinkedQueue Enqueue. The empty case is already written. The problem says that the rest is missing: the new node is not linked in when the queue is not empty, and lastNode is never updated. This is slide 04. Step 2. LinkedQueue Dequeue. Remove and return the front value. The precondition says the queue is not empty. Think about the queue with one node, which is slide 04b. Step 3. Priority Enqueue. The new entry goes to the front instead of the back, so 4 added to 1, 2, 3 gives 4, 1, 2, 3. Draw it on the empty queue first and ask which references have to change. Step 4. Almost-Priority Enqueue. The new entry goes right after the first node, so 4 gives 1, 4, 2, 3. The empty case is handled. Draw the queue with exactly one node and ask what lastNode should be afterwards. Also due this week: Challenge Exercise 08, Carrano Deque, on Friday, which is the deque from today; and Project 2 Deliverable 3, the prototype with tests on at least two element types, at the start of your lab.
