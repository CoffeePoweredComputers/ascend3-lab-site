VOTE 1 · THE ANSWER

Pop takes the top; push(str) puts R back

A walkthrough of the eight operations under a program-counter bar, with the stack drawn beside the code as a column of cells, top up. Push K, push P, push G, push R: four cells grow in, bottom to top. Pop: the R cell fades out and a pill reading str equals R appears beside the column. Pop again: G fades out and nothing keeps it. Push V: a cell holding V grows in on top of P. Push str: a cell holding R grows in on top of V. Then the four options return under the code, A, B and D struck through and C, R V P K, left standing; the caption reads top first, R V P K.

eight operations on an empty stack

  1. stack.push("K");
  2. stack.push("P");
  3. stack.push("G");
  4. stack.push("R");
  5. String str = stack.pop();
  6. stack.pop();
  7. stack.push("V");
  8. stack.push(str);
  1. C · R V P K
  2. A · P V G P
  3. B · K P V R
  4. D · G V P K
Answer: C, R V P K. Steps 1 to 4: four pushes, K P G R, each on top of the last; the drawing is top-up, so the top of the stack is the highest cell and the leftmost letter in the options. Step 5: pop returns the top, R, and the assignment keeps it in str; R leaves the stack. Step 6: pop again, G comes off, and nothing keeps it. Step 7: push V goes on top of P. Step 8: push(str) is the trap: str is R, so R goes back on top. Read down from the top: R V P K. Step 9: C. A reads the stack bottom-up and keeps G; B reads it bottom-up; D forgets that G was popped.
