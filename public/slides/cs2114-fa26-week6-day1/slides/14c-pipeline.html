HOW THIS WILL GO

How your questions get to an ethicist, and back

Five stations in a row, joined by arrows that draw on one per press, each a line drawing over a numeral and a caption. One, a person talking, I introduce a few ideas, today. Two, in orange, a sheet with lines and a pencil, you put your questions in a form, today. Three, two people and a microphone, I interview Rebekah Apple, medical ethicist at CMU Heinz College, with some of your questions. Four, in orange, a screen with a play button, you watch the short clips. Five, three people around a speech bubble, we discuss them, briefly, in class. Under the row, a line: your questions decide what I ask; the form stays open after class.

The plan, five steps. Step 1: today I introduce a few ideas — three ways of arguing, and one word for why they are hard to apply. Step 2: you put your questions in a form, as many as you like, before you leave today; that is the ten minutes at the end.
