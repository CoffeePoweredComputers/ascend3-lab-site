Your concern, as a question

On the left, three numbered steps. One, name something going on in technology and AI that concerns you, with starter chips: AI writing your code, feeds and attention, training data and who labels it, face recognition, a self-driving car’s choices. Two, put it as a question using one of the four words, each with its question in plain speech: outcomes, who ends up better off, who ends up worse off, and is the total worth it; character, what kind of person, or company, does building this make you; duty, would it still be okay if everyone did this, is anyone being used without being asked; moral distance, who never meets the people this lands on, and so never feels it was their decision. Three, or ask about the words themselves, and how they relate to you, a future technologist. On the right, a clock ring reading ten minutes that starts on the slide’s one press, and under it a white plate with a QR code and a link to the form where everyone posts their questions, as many as they like.
