Step 1: something in technology and AI that concerns you — the chips are examples, not the whole list: AI writing your code, feeds, face recognition, the people who label the training data, the self-driving choices from the Moral Machine in 6.1. Step 2: put it as a question using one of the four words along the bottom, each with its framework in front and its question written out: utilitarianism, outcomes, who ends up better off and worse off, and is it worth it; virtue ethics, character, what kind of person or company does this make you; Kantian ethics, duty, what if everyone did it, and is anyone used without their say; moral distance, who never meets the people it lands on. Step 3 is just as good: a question about a word itself — what does utilitarianism actually mean, what is virtue for a team, what does persons as ends mean for a developer who did not choose the feature, what counts as consent, whose decision is it when there are many hands — and how these relate to you as a future technologist.
