How a week runs

A column-labelled list of four rule-separated rows, revealed one per step: when, what, hours. Wednesdays 1:00 to 2:30 — studio: everyone in the room working on their project, together; David wanders, so show him something, get unblocked, pair up; about ninety minutes, all hands. Your own time — independent work on your task; leave every studio with a concrete next one; about three hours, on your schedule. Office hours — one-on-one time with David; a whenisgood goes out this week, mark the slots that work and he picks a standing time; weekly, time set by whenisgood. With your mentor — check-ins with your grad or senior-peer mentor as the work needs it; about an hour, as needed. Final step, an amber-bordered callout: today is the exception — intros, projects, sign-up; from next Wednesday, studio time is working time.
