Sign Up & Access · IRB

Getting cleared to work with data

A horizontal timeline of three steps, revealed one per step: 1, CITI training — the online "Basic Social and Behavioral Research" course, a few hours, taken by everyone on a protocol; 2, added to the protocol — David or your mentor lists you as study personnel on the project's IRB protocol; 3, data and sessions — existing data unlocks, and new think-alouds or sessions run under the approved protocol. A final step reveals an amber-bordered callout: why — research with people, or their data, needs Virginia Tech's Institutional Review Board to sign off; no exceptions for undergrads, and no data before you're on the protocol. Footer: the VT IRB pathfinder on the lab wiki.

Why the extra step

Research with people — or their data — needs VT's Institutional Review Board to sign off. No exceptions for undergrads, and no data before you're on the protocol.

Not sure what your project needs? Lab wiki → IRB → VT IRB pathfinder

IRB = Institutional Review Board, VT's human-subjects oversight. Three steps, in order: CITI (start this week — it's the slow part), then I add you to the protocol, then data. Existing-data projects unlock at step 2; participant-facing projects can't run a session until the protocol covers it. The wiki has a pathfinder for figuring out which category a study falls in.
