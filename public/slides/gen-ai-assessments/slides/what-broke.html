Returning to pedagogy

GenAI has further damaged two kinds of assessment.

In both, the response stops being evidence of what the student can do — and the whole chain of inferences above it goes with it.

1 — Formative assessment

Homework, practice problems, practice exams, short, unproctored quizzes, etc

2 — Unproctored summative

Project-based learning and other take-home, unproctored/invigilated exams.

because the effort offloads to GenAI
