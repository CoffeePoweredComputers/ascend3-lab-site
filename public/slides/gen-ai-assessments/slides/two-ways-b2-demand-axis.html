Irrelevant variance
  • “too broad” — invalidly low or high

Key takeaways

  • Classroom test or homework

    List every skill the course claims to teach (at that point) and then check each one has items.

  • Pre/post intervention test

    All the same considerations as a classroom test but you target the constructs you are measuring to coverage of your intervention.
