Impact of testing frequency

Comparing Frequent and Infrequent Exams

A quasi-experiment comparing frequently tested students in one semester with less frequently tested students in another.

Baseline — midterm + final

Frequent — quiz · exam, alternating

Findings

  • STRESS ↓

    More, smaller exams lowered reported test anxiety.

  • perceived difficulty ↓

    Same questions rated easier when spread across more exams.

  • textbook time ↑

    More time reading, more earnest answers to exercises.
