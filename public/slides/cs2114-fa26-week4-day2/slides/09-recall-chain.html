RECALL · WEEK 4 · LINKED CHAINS

What is myVal?

A recall question on linked chains. Across the top, the chain drawn as on the Day 1 slide: a blue pill labelled head with an arrow into a Link box holding 7, then boxes holding 3, 9 and 4, each reached by a blue arrow from the previous box’s next compartment, the last arrow ending at the word null. Below, on the left, a four-line snippet: Link p equals head; Link q equals head dot next; Link r equals q dot next; Integer myVal equals q dot data. On the right, four options: A, 7; B, 3; C, 9; D, 4. On the one step, the four answers return under both columns, A, C and D struck through in turn and B, 3, left standing.

the snippet

  1. Link p = head;
  2. Link q = head.next;
  3. Link r = q.next;
  4. Integer myVal = q.data;

  1. 7

  2. 3

  3. 9

  4. 4

  1. 7 is p.data
  2. 3 — q is head.next
  3. 9 is r.data
  4. 4 is the last node’s data
Answer: B — 3. Show of hands, then the answer. Nothing is rewired: p refers to the first node, q to head.next (the second), r to q.next (the third); q.data is 3. Field access compiles because Link’s fields are public.
