Propose at least one research question. The first codebook is built to answer the ones the team picks.

## The study starts with

What do students ask about? Build a topic map: a short list of topic areas that every response fits into.

## A usable question

- The data can answer it. There is a topic, a question and a self-chosen lens for each response. There is nothing about who the student is.
- You can name the one or two things to code on each response.

## Ideas

- Which topics draw the most questions?
- Are questions about what is right, what is legal, or what will happen?
- Does the question match the lens the student said they used? The lens is hidden while coding so this stays a fair comparison.
