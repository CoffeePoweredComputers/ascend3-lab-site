Read every response and jot what you notice. Everyone does this for all of them.

- **J** puts the cursor in the jot box. **Ctrl+Enter** saves and goes on.
- **Enter** goes to the next card without a jot.
- **X** flags a response that should not be in the data, then the reason: **1** not about the assignment, **2** blank or a test entry.

A flagged card goes to the lead, who keeps or excludes it. Do not flag these; jot instead:

- a response with several questions in it
- a statement with no question mark
- a very short one, such as "How do we regulate AI"
- one that says the same as another response

Do not fix spelling. A vague question is not a reason to flag.

## Jots

Your jots are private until the merge. Your candidate codes are made from them, so jot on what you would want to code.

- what the question is really about
- what surprised you
- a pattern you have now seen a few times

Jotting closes when you generate your candidate codes in Open coding.

## Lead

Flagged cards are under **with the lead**. Keep or exclude each one. Questions cannot be opened while any are waiting.
