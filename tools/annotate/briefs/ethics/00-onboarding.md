Each card is one question a CS2114 student wrote after a class on ethics in computing, with the topic they gave it.

## The data

- Students named their own topics, so "AI writing code", "ai code" and "Ai" are all in there. The first goal is a topic map: a short list of topic areas.
- They were taught four lenses: outcomes, character, duty, and moral distance.
- Each student also said which lens they used. That answer is hidden from you and kept for comparison later.

## The trail

- Press Start to join. When everyone has, the lead locks the team; nobody joins after that.
- Each waypoint on the left is a stage. The team moves together: the lead opens the next one.
- Every stage has a short guide at the top of its page.
- You gain elevation for each card and memo, never for speed.

## Cards and keys

- Cleaning and coding show one card at a time.
- Every button shows its key. `Enter` is the main action, the arrows move between cards, `Esc` leaves.
- Your jots and codes are yours alone until the lead closes a deck.
- Refer to a card by its six-character id.
