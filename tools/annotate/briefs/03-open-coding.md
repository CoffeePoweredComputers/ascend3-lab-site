Everyone codes the same cards, alone, and builds their own codebook as they go. Wiki: [initial coding](/wiki/qualitative/initial-coding).

## Steps

1. Press **Generate** once. It proposes candidate codes from your own jots and the team's questions, and closes your jotting.
2. The lead deals the same cards to everyone.
3. On each card, tick the codes that apply. **Enter** saves and deals the next card.
4. The lead closes open coding. Your codes are then fixed and go into the merge.

Nobody sees your codes until the lead closes.

## Your codes

- Number keys are your own codes. Letter keys are candidates; ticking one makes it yours.
- **N** starts a new code. Give it a name and a definition. The card you are on becomes its example.
- Rename or redefine a code any time before the close. The change shows on every card it is on.
- Ignore a candidate that does not fit. An unused candidate goes nowhere.

## Good codes

- Describe, do not judge: "asks who is responsible", not "good question".
- Two to five words for the name. One sentence for when it applies.
- Several per card is normal. None is allowed.
- Make a new code when no existing one fits. Do not stretch one to cover it.
