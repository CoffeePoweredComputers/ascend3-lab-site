The final pass. Every kept card is coded with the final codebook, including cards from earlier decks.

## Steps

1. The lead deals the final pass. Most cards go to one person. About one in five goes to two, and nobody is told which.
2. Code your cards, then submit. Submitting locks your codes.
3. The lead closes. The cards two people coded differently are listed under **Review**.
4. Settle each one together. Whoever is driving saves the consensus.
5. The lead opens Themes when every card is settled.

## While coding

- Code as you did in calibration: the definitions decide, not what you think the team would say.
- Do not discuss cards until the lead closes.
- The codebook cannot change during the pass. Note a problem in a memo and keep going.

## The agreement figure

Agreement on the cards coded twice is the number to report for the study. It is frozen when the lead closes, before any consensus is recorded.

A code that was weak in calibration is still weak on the cards one person coded. The figure says how weak. Say so when you report it.
