Two passes. First the team cleans the photos. Then everyone reads every submission and jots.

## 1. Clean

The photos are divided among the team when the lead locks it. You clean yours; each photo is cleaned once.

1. Turn it upright with **[** and **]**. Turn before cropping.
2. Drag a box around the diagram. From then on everyone sees only what is inside.
3. **Enter** keeps it and deals the next photo.

**X** flags a submission that should not be in the data, then the reason: **1** not an attempt at the task, **2** unreadable, **3** identifying and cannot be cropped. Flag with **3** if a face, hand, name, date, clock or other tab is visible and the box cannot leave it out. The student's writing is on the right; flag if it names someone.

A weak or unusual diagram is not a reason to flag. A submission with no diagram stays in for its written parts.

Until a photo is cleaned, whoever opens it sees all of it.

## 2. Read and jot

Starts when every photo is clean. Everyone does this for all of them.

The prompt the student was answering is at the top of each card. Under it are the three sources, each with its own jot box: the **diagram**, the **approach** and the **challenges**. Every source the student handed in needs a jot before the card can be passed. A missing diagram or a blank paragraph has no box.

- **D**, **A** and **C** put the cursor in that source's box. **Ctrl+Enter** saves and goes on.
- A click on the diagram, or **+**, opens it big. **Esc** closes it.
- **X** still flags a card for the lead, and needs no jots.

Your jots are private until the merge. Your candidate codes are made from them, and each keeps the source it was written under, so jot on what you would want to code.

- the diagram: what each box is, and what the lines mean
- the approach: how they say they broke the problem down
- the challenges: what they say was hard
- a pattern you have now seen a few times

Jotting closes when you generate your candidate codes in Open coding.

## Lead

If someone cannot finish their photos, take them off the roster: their photos go to the others. You can also clean anyone's photo yourself.

Flagged cards are under **with the lead**. Crop and keep, or exclude. To fix the crop on a card already in the data, open it and take it back. Questions cannot be opened while any card is waiting or any photo is uncleaned.
