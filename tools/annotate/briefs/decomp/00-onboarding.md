Each card is one submission from an introductory programming course: the diagram a student drew to plan a program, and what they wrote about how it went.

## The data

- Four assignments across a semester: functions, dictionaries, data tables, classes.
- Students used a template: each step has an input, an output and a description.
- The diagram and the reflection are shown together and coded as separate questions.
- Background: *Planning on Paper* (ICER 2026) and *Drawing Your Programs* (arXiv 2602.10529).

## The trail

- Press Start to join. When everyone has, the lead locks the team; nobody joins after that.
- Each waypoint on the left is a stage. The team moves together: the lead opens the next one.
- Every stage has a short guide at the top of its page.
- You gain elevation for each card and memo, never for speed. Your height is matched to a real mountain, on Earth or another world; its name links to Wikipedia.

## Cards and keys

- Cleaning, reading and coding show one card at a time: the photo on the left, what the student wrote on the right.
- Every button shows its key. `Enter` is the main action, the arrows move between cards, `Esc` leaves.
- Your jots and codes are yours alone until the lead closes a deck.
- Refer to a card by its six-character id.
