Propose at least one research question. The first codebook is built to answer the ones the team picks.

Then settle the set as a team. Anyone can reword a question or set it aside; earlier wordings are kept. The set closes for good when the lead unlocks Open coding.

## The study starts with

1. How do students' diagrams change across the semester?
2. What do students struggle with when decomposing a problem?

## A usable question

- The data can answer it. There are diagrams and reflections at four points in time. There are no grades, no code and no interviews.
- It asks how or what, not whether something is good.
- You can name the two or three things to code on each item.

## Format

1. The question.
2. What you would code.
3. What made you ask: item ids or a memo.

To compare a student's first and last diagram, use codes that apply to every assignment.
